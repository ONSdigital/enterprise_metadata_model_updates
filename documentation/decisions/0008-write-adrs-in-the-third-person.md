# 8. Write ADRs in the third person

Date: 12/05/2026 14:10

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

During a metadata model/ontology meeting on 12/5 Charles B noted that the ADRs tend to slip between first and third person, based on however Sam I was feeling at the time she wrote them. This was a bit jarring and possibly funny.

## Decision

That ADRs would be written in third person for consistency, and to not confuse the many future (adoring) developers who will pore over these historical documents.

## Consequences

Samantha I and any other contributors will write ADRs in the third person from ADR 8 onwards.
