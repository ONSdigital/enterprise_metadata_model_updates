# 16. Align domain profile naming to GSBPM

Date: 19/06/2026 16:59

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

The domain profiles were previously named after business areas (DS-DP, DORA etc) but it would make more sense to name them after the GSBPM phase they belong to as all areas touching on that phase will have the same data structures and uses. This also makes it easier for teams in future that operate across more than one GSBPM phase as it will be clear what metadata is required for the datasets within those phases.

## Decision

After correspondence with James A., Charles B, Kirti T et all it was decided to rename the domain profiles to align with the GSBPM phases. Therefore DS-DP domain profile becomes Dissemination domain profile.

## Consequences

Models updated, as this has been caught early it's not likely to have any major knock on effects.
